About

This page collects the things a new resident asks about in the first week: how the house works, who to call, and where to find the laundry room.

House rules

Quiet hours start at 23:00. Guests are signed in at the front desk and leave before midnight. Kitchens are cleaned by the person who used them.

Laundry

Two machines on the first floor. Book a slot on the board near the entrance, one slot per room per day.

Quick tip

Save the administrator number in your phone on the day you move in.
