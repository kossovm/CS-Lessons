Before you start

  • Check the brake lever before leaving the parking zone.
  • One rider per scooter. Two people on one deck is how the fork bends.
  • If the battery shows under 20 percent, pick another scooter.

Where you may ride

  1. Bike lanes first, always.
  2. Pavement only at walking speed, and never on Panfilov street.
  3. Road edge is allowed where there is no lane, keep right.

Parking that does not get you fined

A scooter left across a pavement blocks prams, wheelchairs and everybody else. End the ride in a marked zone, wheels parallel to the kerb, nothing sticking into the walking line. Back to Home.
