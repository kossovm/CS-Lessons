Accessibility notes

What was wrong in before.html and what I fixed here:

  1. Inputs had no labels, only placeholders that disappear on typing. Each field now has a <label for> tied to its id; the scooter number has a hint linked with aria-describedby.
  2. "Send" was a div with onclick — unreachable by Tab, deaf to Enter and Space. It is now a real <button type="submit">.
  3. Grey text on white (#bbb, about 1.9:1) failed contrast. Text now uses the seed dark red at 12:1, labels at over 8:1.
  4. Focus was removed with *:focus { outline: none }. Every link, input and button now has a :focus-visible outline in the seed accent.
  5. Heading chaos — two h1 and a jump to h4. Now one h1, then h2. The brand moved out of the headings into the header.
  6. The logo had no alt. It sits next to the brand name and adds nothing a reader would miss, so it is alt="" — decorative, skipped by screen readers.
  7. No lang, no viewport, no landmarks. Added lang="en", the viewport meta, header / main / footer and a skip link to the form.
